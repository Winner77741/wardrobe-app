AI fills in item details

When you choose a photo in “Add an item”, AI suggests the name, type, colors and dressiness. You always check them before adding. Each photo is sent to Anthropic and costs a fraction of a cent.

Get a key at console.anthropic.com. The key is saved only on this Mac, in the Wardrobe folder's .env file.

Weather

Match outfits to the forecast: no wool on hot days, no sandals in the rain. Only your town's map position is sent to Open-Meteo, a free weather service.

Your whole closet (items, saved outfits, ratings and every photo) in one file. Keep a copy somewhere safe, or use it to move your closet to the iPhone app.
